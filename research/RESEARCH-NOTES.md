# Research scope and verification notes

Research date: 13 September 2026. The research used public web sources. No private employer records, authenticated financial systems or client data were accessed.

The scope covers the two target companies' public offerings, Zoho Books and CRM API integration, native Zoho automation/reporting options, current accounting-agent product direction, practical learning resources, evaluation, data controls and a future practice app. Company pages and product announcements are first-party claims, not independent validation of product performance.

## Material findings

- Prudent describes connected bookkeeping, tax, payroll and CFO services. These support workflow and reporting scenarios; they do not establish its internal stack.
- Zeni's AI Accountant page explicitly identifies QuickBooks Online Plus as its accounting foundation. The curriculum therefore includes QBO adaptation after the Zoho core.
- Zoho Books and CRM require product-specific adapters and quota handling. Books regional domains and organization identity are central to correct requests.
- Zoho Analytics/native workflows and built-in accounting AI should be considered before building equivalent custom features.
- Agent evaluation needs final-state checks and business evidence, not just prose quality. The proposed learning system makes these visible.

## Access limitations and uncertainty

1. Zoho Books' invoices reference was available through a detailed search-index excerpt but returned an error on direct fetch. The general pagination documentation and invoice sample differ in illustrated page_context shape; the plan explicitly requires checking the actual resource response.
2. The DeepLearning.AI Agentic AI description was accessible in search results, but the full course page failed retrieval. It is optional. Exact access/payment terms are not represented as verified.
3. Intuit's QBO sandbox, webhook and change-data-capture pages returned a JavaScript shell. The sandbox link is retained as a setup entry point only. Exact current QBO setup, scopes, quotas and CDC behaviour must be verified in the developer portal during the later lab; the plan does not invent them.
4. The old n8n learning-path URL returned a moved/not-found page. The plan points to the current n8n Academy course catalog, which was accessible. Specific paid/free course promises are avoided.
5. No verified role description, hiring requirements or internal process inventory was supplied. Recommendations are tailored to public service/product context and the stated Zoho goal, not to an asserted vacancy.
6. Product feature availability varies by account and release. Zoho help explicitly marks certain AI field features early access. A feature documented on a vendor site is not assumed enabled for every customer.

## Evidence conventions

Stable IDs S1-S36 map to sources.json and the source tags in the Word footnotes. Sources include exact URLs, publishers, titles, available publication dates and access notes. Living documents are labelled as such instead of inventing a publication date. The main plan's scenarios, curriculum, evaluation thresholds, learning-app rules and effort estimates are recommendations.

No vendor time-saving, accuracy or adoption statistic is used as a promised learner outcome. No real API call, prototype, learner test or app deployment is claimed in the planning release. The 20-week plan totals 400 study hours and separates learner time from future builder effort.
